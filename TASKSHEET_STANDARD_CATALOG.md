**AHS / Continuing-Care Aligned Task & Unit Routine Starter Templates**
*Developed by SoftVibeSolutions*

## 1. Overview & Disclaimer

The **Alberta Starter Catalog** provides pre-configured, evidence-informed task templates tailored for Alberta continuing-care teams (Health Care Aides, Licensed Practical Nurses, and Registered Nurses).

> [!NOTE]
> Catalog entries serve as workflow starter templates. Clinical assignments remain governed by individual resident care plans, physician/prescriber orders, facility policy, and applicable professional regulatory frameworks.
