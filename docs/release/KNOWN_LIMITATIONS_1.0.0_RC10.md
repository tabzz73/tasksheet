# Known Limitations - TaskSheet 1.0.0-rc.10

- City suggestions cover common Canadian municipalities and permit free entry for all others; TaskSheet does not perform online address verification.
- Phone formatting targets Canadian/NANP 10-digit numbers with optional `+1`; international facility numbers are not normalized.
- RC10 remains subject to clean-machine and controlled-pilot approval.
- The installer is unsigned; verify its published SHA-256.
